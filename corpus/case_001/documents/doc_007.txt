THE ROSS AND HEREFORD TIMES — LOCAL NEWS CLIPPING
"BOSCOMBE VALLEY TRAGEDY — SON HELD FOR FATHER'S MURDER"
(unsigned, staff correspondent)

Our readers will have followed with horror the events at Boscombe Pool, where Mr. Charles McCarthy, a well-known and respected tenant of Mr. John Turner's Hatherley estate, met his death under violent circumstances. We are reliably informed that the case against the younger McCarthy is all but conclusive: witnesses observed the two men quarrelling bitterly shortly before the elder Mr. McCarthy was found dying, and it is widely reported in the village that young McCarthy raised his hand against his own father not an hour before the body was discovered. Coupled with his initial, and frankly damning, denial to Mr. Crowder that he had seen his father at all that afternoon, there can be little doubt in the public mind as to the true course of events. The presence of his gun at the scene, though undischarged, has been the subject of much local speculation as to the true weapon used. It is understood the young man's motive lay in a long-standing dispute with his father over his attachment to a young lady of the district, whose name we withhold out of decency. Barring some remarkable turn, the inquest is expected to be a formality.
