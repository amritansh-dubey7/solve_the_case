VILLAGE GOSSIP NOTE — RELAYED SECOND-HAND TO THE CONSTABULARY
Recorded by Constable Figgins from a conversation with a Hatherley Farm labourer, not a direct witness to any relevant event.

Word around the village has it that young James McCarthy has "a temper on him" and was seen a year or two back in a public house scuffle with another farmhand over some trifling matter, though no charges came of it and nobody could quite agree on who struck the first blow, or indeed whether it happened at all as told. Several versions of the story circulate, each a little worse than the last. No one relaying this claims to have witnessed the alleged scuffle personally; it is offered as "the sort of thing folk are saying" rather than as direct testimony.

[Constabulary note: hearsay, uncorroborated by any named witness. Filed as unverified.]
