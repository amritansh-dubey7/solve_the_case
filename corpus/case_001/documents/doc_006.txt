PHYSICAL EVIDENCE REPORT — BOSCOMBE POOL SITE EXAMINATION
Conducted by Mr. Sherlock Holmes at the request of Inspector Lestrade, with Constable Figgins present
Date: two days after the death (ground still soft from recent rain)

The ground near the pool retains excellent impressions owing to the recent rain and soft clay soil.

1. James McCarthy's footprints are present approaching and leaving the pool from the direction of the warren, consistent with his account.

2. A second, separate set of footprints was found on the far side of the pool, approaching from the opposite direction to James McCarthy's — that is, from the direction of the Boscombe Valley estate house rather than the farm or warren. These prints show an uneven gait: the right foot's impression is markedly deeper and the stride shorter on that side, consistent with a limp or a stiff leg. A series of small round punctures alongside these footprints, spaced consistently with the stride, are consistent with the tip of a walking-stick or cane used for support.

3. A fragment of grey woollen cloth, torn, was recovered from a thorn bush at approximately the height of a tall man's shoulder, along the path taken by the second set of footprints, roughly forty yards from the pool. The cloth appears to be from a heavy travelling-cloak or overcoat, not a coat of the kind McCarthy or his son are known to own.

4. Ash consistent with a strong, dark cigar — not commonly smoked by either McCarthy — was found at a spot approximately twenty yards from the pool, at a position from which the pool would have been visible through the reeds, suggesting someone stood and watched before or after the events at the pool.

5. No weapon was recovered at the scene. A heavy, rounded grey stone consistent in size with the injuries was found in the reeds near the water but showed no definitive traces linking it to the wounds; it has been retained as a possible weapon.

Conclusion: the second set of footprints, the torn grey cloak fragment, and the cigar ash together indicate a second person, tall, with a pronounced limp or stiffness in the right leg, using a stick, wearing a grey cloak, and smoking dark cigars, was present at or very near the pool at the relevant time — a person distinct from James McCarthy.
