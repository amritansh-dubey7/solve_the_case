WITNESS STATEMENT — MRS. HANNAH LAXTON, proprietress of the Hereford Arms public house, Ross-on-Wye
Taken by Constable Figgins

Mr. John Turner is well known to me as a regular, if infrequent, customer for cigars — I keep a small stock of the strong dark Trichinopoly kind especially because he asks for them, as few others in these parts smoke them. He called in for a fresh box some four or five days before Mr. McCarthy's death, and I recall remarking to my husband afterward how unwell and shaky he looked, worse than his usual self, and how heavily he leaned on his stick even to cross the room to the bar. He wore his grey travelling-cloak that day, as he most always does now on account of feeling the cold.

I did not see him in the village on the day of the death itself, but then I would not necessarily have, as my afternoons are given over to the kitchen and I do not always mark who passes on the road.
