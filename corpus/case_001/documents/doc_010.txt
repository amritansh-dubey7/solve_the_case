LETTER FRAGMENT — JOHN TURNER, DRAFT (UNSENT)
Recovered from a wastepaper basket at the Boscombe Valley estate house, torn but partly legible; date uncertain, believed several weeks before the death.

...cannot go on paying in this coin forever, McCarthy. You ask now for Alice herself, my own daughter, to settle what you say I owe you, and I tell you I would sooner be exposed and ruined, and see myself in the dock, than sell her to your son to buy your silence. There must be an end to this. I am not the man I was, and I have not long in any case, whatever you may say to hurry the reckoning...

[Remainder illegible/torn away.]
