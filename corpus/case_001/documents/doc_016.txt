EXHIBIT DESCRIPTION — TORN CLOAK FRAGMENT
Catalogued by Constable Figgins, cross-referenced to physical evidence report doc_006

A single fragment of heavy grey woollen cloth, approximately three inches square, torn irregularly along one edge as though caught and pulled free from a thorn bush rather than cut. The weave and dye are consistent with a gentleman's travelling-cloak of a somewhat old-fashioned, heavy pattern more commonly seen ten to fifteen years ago, of a quality suggesting a wealthy owner rather than a labourer or vagrant. The fragment has been retained for comparison against any cloak produced in evidence. It does not match any garment known to belong to Charles McCarthy or James McCarthy, both of whom own only lighter-weight coats appropriate to farm work.
