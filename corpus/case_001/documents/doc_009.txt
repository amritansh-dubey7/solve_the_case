LETTER — CHARLES McCARTHY TO JOHN TURNER
Found among the deceased's papers at Hatherley Farm, undated but believed written the morning of his death.
(Draft copy retained by McCarthy; original presumed delivered by hand.)

Turner,

Meet me at the pool this afternoon at three. You know why. I have waited long enough and I will not be put off again — the boy and the girl are to be married, and that is my price, as it has always been. Do not think of refusing me now, of all times, when you are less able than ever to bear what I could say, and to whom.

C. McC.
