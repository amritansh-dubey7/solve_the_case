HEREFORDSHIRE CONSTABULARY — INITIAL RESPONSE REPORT
Case: Death of Charles McCarthy, Boscombe Pool, Boscombe Valley
Reporting Officer: Constable H. Figgins, Ross Division
Date of Incident: 3 June

At approximately 3:45 p.m. I was summoned to Boscombe Pool, a small lake bordered by reeds at the edge of a wood on the Hatherley/Boscombe Valley estate, by Mr. John Cobb, a laborer, who stated a body had been found in the water at the pool's edge.

On arrival I found the deceased, later identified as Charles McCarthy, tenant farmer of Hatherley Farm, lying face down partly in the shallows. The skull had sustained severe blunt-force injuries to the back and side of the head. No firearm wound was present. A heavy stone was later recovered nearby but not confirmed as the weapon.

The deceased's son, James McCarthy, was found at the scene by William Crowder (gamekeeper on the estate) and Mrs. Moran (wife of the lodge-keeper), who had been drawn to the pool by a cry. James McCarthy was in a distressed state and was carrying a double-barrelled gun. He was taken into custody pending inquest on the strength of witness accounts of a quarrel between father and son shortly before the death (see doc_002, doc_003).

James McCarthy initially told Mr. Crowder he had not seen his father that afternoon. He later admitted this was false, stating he lied "out of shame," not guilt (see doc_004).

Status: James McCarthy remanded in custody. Inquest scheduled. Mr. Sherlock Holmes, consulting detective, has been engaged by Miss Alice Turner, a family friend, to review the case independently at the request of Inspector Lestrade.
