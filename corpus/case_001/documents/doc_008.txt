ANONYMOUS LETTER — RECEIVED BY ROSS CONSTABULARY
Postmarked Ross-on-Wye, delivered the day after the inquest opened. Handwriting disguised; unsigned.

You are all looking the wrong way. Ask about the tramp who was seen begging on the Ross road two days before the murder, a rough fellow with a bundle, who was turned away from Hatherley Farm by McCarthy himself the very morning of the killing after asking for money and being refused. A man like that, desperate and shamed, is your murderer, not the son. Look to the tramp and you will find your answer. I know what I know and will say no more.

[Constabulary note: no tramp matching this description has been located or corroborated by any other witness. Source and reliability unknown. Filed as unverified.]
