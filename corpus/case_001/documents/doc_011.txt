DIARY ENTRY — ALICE TURNER
Personal diary, entry dated some weeks before the death, shown voluntarily to Mr. Holmes.

Father was so ill again tonight after Mr. McCarthy called. He will not tell me what passes between them, only that "old debts" must be paid, and that I am not to ask more. I cannot understand why Father, who owns half the valley and has always been so generous to the McCarthys — forgiving Mr. McCarthy his rent these many years, letting him farm Hatherley for next to nothing — should go so pale and shaking whenever that man's name is mentioned, as though he were the tenant and Mr. McCarthy the landlord, and not the other way about.

James is the dearest man alive and I love him, but I cannot marry him while his father treats mine so, holding some threat over him that I am not permitted to know. I begged Father to tell me what it is, and he only said, "Better you never know, Alice. It is an old wrong, from before you were born, from the other side of the world, and it is mine to answer for, not yours."

I am so afraid for him. He grows weaker by the month, and Dr. Anstruther says his kidneys are failing and he has not long. It seems a cruelty beyond bearing that he should spend what time he has left tormented like this.
