OLD COLONIAL NEWSPAPER CUTTING — "THE BALLARAT COURIER" (AUSTRALIA), REPRINTED YEARS AGO
Found folded inside a locked drawer at the Boscombe Valley estate house among Mr. Turner's private papers; date on the cutting is from approximately twenty-two years before the present case.

GOLD ESCORT ATTACKED — ONE GUARD SLAIN — BUSHRANGERS SUSPECTED

A gold convoy travelling from the Ballarat diggings under armed escort was set upon by a party of mounted bushrangers three miles outside the township. One guard was shot and killed in the exchange; the convoy's gold was seized. Colonial police believe the notorious gang led by the man known locally as "Black Jack" was responsible, the same party suspected in at least two prior robberies in the district. Several members of the escort survived and may be able to identify members of the gang if apprehended. The gang has not been apprehended to date and is believed to have dispersed, several members reportedly having taken ship for England or elsewhere to escape pursuit.

[A handwritten note in the margin, believed to be in Mr. Turner's hand: "C. McC. was one of the escort. He knows my face."]
