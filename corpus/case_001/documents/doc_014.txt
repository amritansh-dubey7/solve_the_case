CONSTABULARY EXHIBIT REPORT — JAMES McCARTHY'S GUN
Examined by Constable Figgins with the assistance of a Ross gunsmith

The double-barrelled gun recovered near the pool, identified as belonging to James McCarthy, was found to be fully loaded in both barrels, neither having been discharged. There is no indication the weapon was fired that day. The gun shows no blood, hair, or tissue on the stock or barrels consistent with use as a bludgeon. This is consistent with James McCarthy's account that he had set the gun aside before approaching his father and that it was not used in the attack.

The wounds described in the surgeon's report (doc_005) are, in the gunsmith's opinion, inconsistent with a blow from a gun of this type in any case, being too broad and rounded for the barrel or stock.
